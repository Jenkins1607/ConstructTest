⚡ Quick Setup (5 Steps)

Step 1: Customize Colors

Open css/styles.css and replace these placeholders (lines 6-9):

  • [Primary color hex] → Your primary color (e.g., #3498db)
  • [Accent color hex] → Your accent color (e.g., #e74c3c)
  • [Neutral color hex] → Your neutral color (e.g., #2c3e50)

Step 2: Update Site Purpose

Find and replace [Site purpose] in all HTML files with your actual site name.

Step 3: Install XAMPP

Download from https://www.apachefriends.org/ and install. Start Apache and MySQL.

Step 4: Setup Database

  1. Open http://localhost/phpmyadmin
  2. Click "Import" and select db/db.sql
  3. Update database credentials in php/contact.php

Step 5: Add Your Images

Place your images in assets/images/:

  • placeholder.jpg
  • about-placeholder.jpg
  • team-1.jpg, team-2.jpg, team-3.jpg
  • portfolio-1.jpg through portfolio-9.jpg
